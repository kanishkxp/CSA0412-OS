=== Alternative: 32 GB RAM, program of 24 GB ===
24 GB = 6291456 pages. With enough RAM every page is brought in once (cold start) and then never again.
  cold-start minor faults at 1.5 us (measured, anonymous memory)  : 9.4 s once
  steady state EAT with 32 GB                                     : 100 ns (p = 0)
  with 16 GB RAM the same program has p > 0 after the first 16 GB is used:
     p = 1e-03 : EAT =    8099.9 ns (8 ms) ->   81.0 x slower than 32 GB
     p = 1e-05 : EAT =     180.0 ns (8 ms) ->    1.8 x slower than 32 GB
     p = 1e-06 : EAT =     108.0 ns (8 ms) ->    1.1 x slower than 32 GB
